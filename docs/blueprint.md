# **App Name**: LuxeBloom

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog: Showcase a curated selection of 3-5 high-quality beauty products with optimized titles, clear descriptions, proper images, and variant options.
- AI-Powered Product Descriptions: Generate concise and compelling product descriptions using AI, focusing on benefits and key features, while also taking advantage of search-engine optimization techniques to maximize their visibility to potential customers.
- Customer Reviews: Integrate and display customer reviews to build trust and social proof.
- Bundled Offers: Create and display product bundles with discount incentives to increase average order value.
- Email Capture: Implement a pop-up form and announcement bar to collect email addresses for marketing and promotional deals.
- Subscription Service: Offer a subscription service for frequently purchased items

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep berry (#8A2BE2) for sophistication and allure.
- Background color: Light lavender (#F0E6FF), a softer shade of the primary.
- Accent color: A muted magenta (#C71585) to highlight sales and calls to action.
- Font pairing: 'Belleza' (sans-serif) for headlines and 'Alegreya' (serif) for body text, for an elegant, readable combination.
- Minimalist icons related to beauty and skincare, used consistently throughout the site.
- Clean and modern layout with clear product galleries and straightforward navigation.
- Subtle animations, such as fading effects when transitioning between images or loading new content, will add a touch of elegance to the user experience.
